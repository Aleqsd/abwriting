Rédactrice / Traductrice / Enseignante chez ABwriting

I’ve been working for the past two years, first as a teacher at my university and then as a free lance content writer and translator for several companies and associations. I am a passionate writer and languages in the broder sense of the term fascinate me. I am detail oriented and my number one focus is to adapt my skills to specific needs. Writing, translating, learning … We can talk about it, let me help!
